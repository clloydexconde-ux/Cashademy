About Cashademy

Cashademy is a student-first platform that empowers learners to earn while they study. It offers opportunities like selling notes, tutoring, creative showcases, micro-tasks, and a preloved marketplace. With EduQuest gamification, students earn points, rewards, and discounts while engaging in learning.
